Abstract
Multi-camera surveillance produces more footage than operators can review. This system detects, recognizes, and tracks faces across cameras with no enrolment. A fine-tuned YOLOv11-nano detector and an ArcFace recognizer run a CPU-only pipeline, resolving each person to one anonymous identity via a central Qdrant gallery. A self-trained ViT fragmented across pose, justifying the chosen pose-robust CNN.
Problem Statement & Objectives
Problem
One operator cannot watch many feeds or cross-reference faces over time. The system must detect every face, assign a stable identity, and answer "when and where did this person appear?" — across 2+ cameras, in real time on CPU, no enrolment.
Objectives
  • Detect faces in real time across 2+ cameras on CPU (YOLOv11-nano).
  • Match each person to one identity across cameras and pose, without enrolment (ArcFace + Qdrant).
  • Persist identities/timelines; serve a live dashboard (timeline, search-by-photo).
Design Constraints
Economic Open-source only; runs on existing PCs — no GPU purchase
Environmental CPU inference + on-demand preview encoding limit energy use
Safety No physical actuation; risk is informational (privacy)
Ethical Anonymous-by-default IDs; demographic bias acknowledged
Sustainability Modular, local-only design; low-cost deployment
Social Privacy-by-design; labels optional, never required

Ethical & Societal Impact
Safety & Health
No physical-actuation risk; the principal risk is informational (privacy), mitigated by local-only storage.
Environmental
CPU inference and on-demand preview encoding limit energy use versus always-on GPU processing.
Ethical
Anonymous person_NNN IDs, no names required; biometric data stays local; inherited demographic bias acknowledged.
Societal Benefit
Security monitoring, access analytics, and missing-person search — balanced against surveillance and privacy risk.
Conclusions & Future Work
1
Detection met: YOLOv11-nano detector (precision 0.873) runs in real time on CPU across multiple cameras.
2
Recognition met: pose-robust ArcFace CNN (+0.31–0.63 cosine) holds one identity across pose; a self-trained ViT (≈ 0) fragmented.
3
Future work: GPU / batched model server for many HD cameras, server-push (SSE / WebSocket) UI, and stronger tracking (ByteTrack / DeepSORT).
